# Executive Summary: Sales Performance Analysis (2015-2018)

## 1. Project Overview
This final project summarizes the findings from a four-week data analytics internship focusing on sales optimization. The goal was to clean raw data, analyze regional and category performance, and identify long-term growth trends.

## 2. Key Business Insights
* **Product Leadership:** Technology is the top-performing category, contributing the highest total sales volume.
* **Regional Dominance:** The West and East regions are the company's primary markets, representing 31.4% and 29.6% of total sales, respectively.
* **Growth Trajectory:** The business has shown significant year-over-year growth, with peak sales performance consistently occurring in the fourth quarter (Q4).

## 3. Data Strategy
To ensure accuracy, all data was cleaned of null values, date formats were standardized, and a unified naming convention was applied to prevent computational errors during the analysis.

## 4. Final Recommendations
Based on the seasonal spikes identified in the trend analysis, it is recommended that the marketing team increase promotional efforts in the West region during Q3 to prepare for the annual Q4 surge.
